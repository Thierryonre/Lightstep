# SOURCE: Hooker et al., "Multi-pulse laser wakefield acceleration" J. Phys. B 47, 234003 (2014)

- DOI 10.1088/0953-4075/47/23/234003 (open access, CC BY 3.0)
- arXiv: https://arxiv.org/abs/1401.7874 (v1, 30 Jan 2014)
- Searched: 2026-10-27

## Result: no public simulation inputs/code

- The paper used weakly-relativistic electron fluid equations (in-house) and OSIRIS PIC; no input decks,
  data or code are linked from the arXiv record or the open-access IOP article.
- OSIRIS itself was not open source in 2014 (open-sourced 2023 at https://github.com/osiris-code), but the
  2014 decks were never published.
- Nothing downloaded. Parameters needed to rebuild the case are in
  docs/research/resonant-multipulse.md section 2 (10 mJ, 100 fs, 1 µm, w0 = 40 µm, a0 = 0.052,
  n_e = 1.75e17 cm^-3, N = 120, spacing = T_pe).
