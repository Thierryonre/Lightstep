"Dispersion-engineered multipass optical parametric amplification", Nature **647**, 74–79 (2025). DOI: 10.1038/s41586-025-09665-w.

## Simulation code and data: NOT public
- Article statements: "The data supporting the findings of this study are available from the corresponding author upon request." and "The code used to produce the results are available from the corresponding author upon request." Contact: tobias.steinle@pi4.uni-stuttgart.de.
- The SI says the pulse-shortening and spectral-broadening check used a 1+1D **pyNLO** simulation (pump 1030 nm, 130 fs; seed 1700 nm, 160 fs; 1 mW seed; 3 W pump at 76 MHz; 50 µm pump waist; idler removed and 110 fs group-delay offset after each 1 mm crystal pass). pyNLO is open source (https://github.com/pyNLO/PyNLO). Not downloaded.
